## Non-negotiable runtime safety
- In Codex, use native workspace, terminal and editing tools; do not invoke external desktop/tunnel connectors or spawn another Codex CLI process. No game-client injection, executors, modified clients, memory/cookie/session access, anti-cheat bypass or blind scripted bypasses.
- Keep exactly **one** guarded Roblox input owner. When a TDS Macro worker runs, use passive status/log/screenshot observation only: no second controller, GUI clicks into Roblox, focus changes, overlays or cursor contamination. Do not build/install over a running worker.
- Supported baseline: Windows 11, official Store Roblox GDK, **1920×991 physical client at 120 DPI**, English game/OCR, `UI Scale: Large`, vertical upgrades, 1× speed, unchanged controls and pinned camera/map/terrain checks.
- Start only after verified lobby, exact ordered tower names/slots, Golden perk where required, Solo/mode/map and supported geometry. **Auto-Equip is unavailable**; visual portraits/borders or empty-looking tiles never prove equipment. Do not silently change user presets or overwrite loadouts.
- Every placement/upgrade must verify live selected tower, level/count, cash, exact price, affordability, valid preview, camera, foreground and positive postcondition. Never guess a coordinate/price or repeat an uncertain paid action. Detect a verified result before more spending.
- Preserve **F1 Start, F2 safe stop, F8/Escape emergency stop**, cancellation during waits, held-input cleanup and the session stop marker. A changed/unknown process, terrain, identity, focus or game state stops input.
- Recovery is **bounded** (up to three guarded relaunches without the required success), uses only a positively identified original Roblox window and official launch flow, never force-kills, blindly retries, steals focus from an active desktop or resumes an uncertain purchase. Follow `src/tds_auto/recovery.py` and evidence logs.
- Keep gameplay OCR for cash/price/tower/result checks. Wretched Front uses **one pinned RIGHT→DOWN→LEFT route with terrain and final three-landmark consensus**, **no automatic game-version OCR or alternate route guessing**.
