### BUG-001 — Golden Scout count 10 is deliberately excluded from pixel templates

- **Priority/status:** **P1 — OPEN, root cause confirmed, fix not implemented.**
- **Impact:** A tenth Scout can be placed successfully, but its `10/40` count becomes unreadable; the action cannot be confirmed and a healthy match is abandoned/relaunched. This unnecessarily loses match progress and creates repeated recoveries.
- **Frequency:** **Six** saved identical failed cases across the two November 23 Golden sessions: old session `app_20261123_104514_298820/attempt_001..003`, earlier session `app_20261123_162916_410440/attempt_001,002,009`. Each stops at **33/50 confirmed actions**, nine recorded Scouts and Scout 9 at level 0; the next scripted action (**34**) is `SpawnTower(..., tower 10)`.
- **Reproduction/evidence:** In every failed attempt's `latest.png`, the local `HudReader` returns **cash readable**, **wave 14 readable**, **Scout / level 0**, but **tower_count=None**. Standard OCR for count ROI reads `10140` or `10 140` (the separator in `10/40` is misread). The **existing** `assets/ui_2191/count_10.png` exactly matches a failed live count crop at **overlap 1.00**, with the next closest template at 0.934, but `HudReader.__init__` only loads count templates `<=9` for Golden. The log's emitted stop is `Unreadable cash or tower count; no resource guessing.`
- **Source:** `src/tds_auto/resources.py`: `HudReader.__init__`, `count_from_pixels`, `read`; `src/tds_auto/match.py:MatchRunner.checked`; `strategies/GOLDIE SCOUTO.vertical-ten-slot1-v031-2202.strat` actions 33–34. Saved captures remain private under `dist/TDSMacro/captures/runs`.
- **Fix direction:** Extend Golden's validated **complete-fraction** pixel-template coverage to include its native-proven `10/40`, not an OCR digit repair, speculative count inference, or a changed paid-action retry. Prefer reusing the existing hash-controlled template only after verifying geometry/false-positive rejection for Golden and Hardcore; maintain current strict OCR/confirmation fallback.
- **Required acceptance:** Red→green regression with all six saved native failing frames; separately verify counts 9, 10, 11/other, missing slash and obscured/shifted HUD do not misidentify as 10; retain no repeated unconfirmed purchase input; native Golden match reaches 50/50 and wave-20 Victory across multiple matches without this stop, followed by one full suite and frozen/native checks before release.

### BUG-002 — Intermittent Golden fresh-camera binding fails after otherwise successful lobby/map setup

- **Priority/status:** **P1 — OPEN, failure mechanism confirmed; physical origin of pose variation not yet confirmed.**
- **Impact:** Fresh Dead Ahead match setup terminates before a placement and forces a client relaunch. A **cluster of three consecutive relaunches** occurred without a win before later recovery succeeded; further starts failed the next morning. Reliability risk is high.
- **Frequency:** **Eight** failed fresh setups across two sessions: five in `app_20261123_162916_410440/attempt_003,004,006,007,008` and three in `app_20261124_045640_615435/attempt_001,002,003`. The later native frames show `CameraGate.estimate=None` for attempts 001/002 and a **10.49px placement-point displacement** (above the unchanged 10px limit) for attempt 003. Nearby later frames passed, so the failure is intermittent.
- **Evidence from saved `latest.png` using the actual `CameraGate` offline:**
  - attempt_003: scale **1.00312**, angle **-0.030°**, max placement-bound displacement **10.71 px**, `bind=False`, `zoom_step=None`.
  - attempt_004: scale **1.00249**, angle **-0.049°**, displacement **11.06 px**, `bind=False`, `zoom_step=None`.
  - attempt_006: scale **1.00352**, angle **-0.044°**, displacement **10.60 px**, `bind=False`, `zoom_step=None`.
  - attempt_007: scale **0.99682**, angle **-0.017°**, displacement **11.08 px**, `bind=False`, `zoom_step=None`.
  - attempt_008: SIFT/RANSAC `estimate=None`; no safe correction can be inferred.
  - Successful nearby `prepared.png` frames bound correctly at approximately **6.05–9.29 px** displacement.
- **Mechanism:** `CameraGate.bind` requires a **maximum 10-px transformed placement-point displacement**. `CameraGate.zoom_step` only proposes correction for centered zoom variation, and explicitly returns `None` when `abs(scale-1)<0.005` or translation is outside its bound. - **Source:** `src/tds_auto/calibration.py`: `CameraGate.estimate`, `bind`, `zoom_step`; `src/tds_auto/session.py`: `prepare_camera` and `align_fresh_camera`.
- **Fix direction:** Investigate why the physical camera/character/movement leaves a small translated pose, using native setup frames and scene landmarks. Evaluate a **bounded, observed, read-only alignment/correction** or a precise verified camera-setup adjustment. **Do not simply enlarge the 10-px placement safety envelope, rebind during gameplay, omit terrain proof, or retry a guessed mouse coordinate.** The RANSAC-insufficient case needs separate diagnosis, not the same arbitrary correction.
- **Required acceptance:** Native and saved-frame positive/negative tests for centered, 10.6–11.1px displaced, missing-feature, rotated and deliberately shifted poses; any correction must end with the unchanged `CameraGate.bind` 10px condition and preserve the 2px in-match drift guard. ### BUG-004 — Golden Scout unconditionally deselects and reselects before every upgrade

- **Priority/status:** **P1 — OPEN, code-level cause confirmed; optimization unimplemented.** It is a recurring interaction/efficiency defect observed by the user, **not yet proven to cause a specific failed match**.
- **Exact source:** `src/tds_auto/match.py:MatchRunner.upgrade` calls `driver.click(1450,500)` followed by `driver.click(*point)` for *every* Scout upgrade. `src/tds_auto/run.py:run_sequence` knows the tower ID but supplies only point, prior level and count; the runner has no per-match selected-tower identity. `use_e_upgrades=True` changes **only the purchase to E**, not the redundant reselection.
- **Scope/measurement:** Golden 10 has **40 upgrades and 80 unconditional selection mouse clicks per complete 50-action match**. There are **23 immediately same-tower transitions** (9 placement→upgrade plus 14 upgrade→upgrade), which may need **zero** selection clicks when native panel state/identity is retained; 17 upgrade targets differ from their immediately preceding action. Potential reductions are **unmeasured**, not guaranteed speed savings. Golden 9's 35 upgrades use legacy mouse purchase and require separate compatibility treatment.
- **Hardcore contrast:** `src/tds_auto/hardcore_match.py:HardcoreMatch.select` remembers `self.selected` and skips a selection click **only** when exact tower/level/count readbacks agree; `HardcoreMatch.upgrade` sends a single guarded E after a current price/cash check. This is the pattern to reuse, without copying its separate gameplay route.
- **Safe fix direction:** Add a **per-match verified selection ID** in the existing Golden path. Reuse a still-proven same tower; for an actually different/unknown target, select only with verified ownership and positive readback. Ensure same-level Scouts cannot be confused. Preserve fresh affordability, expected level/count/camera, one E, post-E confirmation, and the fail-closed no-repeat-purchase guarantee. Keep Golden 9's legacy route unchanged initially.
- **Acceptance:** New targeted tests for place→E, E→E, cross-tower selection, lost/unknown panel, duplicate-level Scouts, expired cash, focus stop, result arrival, no repeated E, and unchanged strategy steps/hashes. Native supervised matches must document **actual** selection-click savings without reduced 50/50 Victory reliability.
