> **Implementation checkpoint, November 25.** The local candidate carries Scout selection provenance through confirmed purchases. Native Golden 10 matches used 34 selection clicks and 40 E upgrades, completed all 50 actions and credited 450 Coins per verified Victory, including a full match via Play Again. The original Scout-obscured Victory verifies in saved-frame replay. A native Victory with a cropped Coin caption stayed uncredited and led to guarded recovery. See [current verification](../verification.md) for exact scope. The sections below retain the original investigation and acceptance criteria.

**Goal:** Eliminate Golden Scout's unconditional deselect/reselect before an already-selected tower's upgrade, reuse the guarded `E` upgrade workflow that Hardcore has proven, and prevent selected-Scout hover/panel overlays from hiding a verified Victory. Preserve all live-input, camera, result and purchase safety gates.

**Current baseline:** v0.5.1; `Golden Scout — 10 Max` (50 actions: ten placements + forty `E` upgrades), plus a separate older `Golden Scout — 9 Scouts` fallback that still uses mouse upgrade clicks. The current v1.0 rotation/preset switch is not part of this patch.

## Investigated behavior / why it happens

- `src/tds_auto/match.py:MatchRunner.upgrade` unconditionally calls `driver.click(1450,500)` and `driver.click(*point)` **for every upgrade**. Those clicks first dismiss an existing tower selection, then reselect the target, before the current guarded live cash/price and one `E` key.
- `src/tds_auto/run.py:run_sequence` knows the exact strategy tower ID, ordered steps, point and confirmed level, but passes only its point/level/count to `MatchRunner.upgrade`. The Golden match runner does **not** retain which tower was last positively selected. Placement positively confirms Scout level 0 and auto-selects the new tower, yet this useful selection is discarded at the next upgrade.
- `src/tds_auto/hardcore_match.py:HardcoreMatch.select` caches `self.selected=tower.tower_id`, reuses the correct selection when the readback agrees, and uses `E` only after live validation. Use this **behavior**, not a new input framework or wholesale Hardcore executor copy.
- Golden 10 has **40 upgrades; 80 unconditional selection clicks** today. Of its upgrade transitions, **23** immediately follow work on the *same* tower (9 placement→upgrade and 14 upgrade→upgrade); the other **17** switch targets. Conditional selection could avoid 46 of the current clicks for proven same-target cases, with further improvement if a single direct cross-target click is natively safe. **These are static input counts, not measured speed savings.**
- The older 9-Scout profile has 35 upgrades and a different legacy mouse-upgrade policy. Preserve it unchanged initially to avoid introducing a second untested behavioral change.

### New native result failure that the revamp must cover

- Session `app_20261124_045640_615435/attempt_004/match_0001` confirmed **50/50 actions**, **10 level-4 Scouts**, and the saved `latest.png` visibly shows **TRIUMPH**, Easy / Survival / Dead Ahead, wave **20**, **450 Coins**, and Play Again. The right-hand selected-Scout hover/panel overlays part of the title.
- Offline `Observer.observe` returned **`in_match` at 1.0**; Victory anchor scored **0.77085**, `result_location=None`. Title OCR `'•TRIUMP Your'` fails the exact `TRIUMPH!` fallback; Play Again OCR succeeded and duration independently read **456 s**. `ResultReader.read` returned `None`. The result was not credited; after **900 seconds** `Playback.play` stopped with `Timed out waiting for a verified match result`, then recovered.
- This is a **separate result detection defect (BUG-005)**, not evidence that the player lost the match. The selection revamp may reduce cursor hover at the tower, but **must not be assumed to solve result detection automatically**.

## Proposed minimal design

### 1. Selection provenance, not blind cached assumptions (BUG-004)

- Keep one **per-match** selected tower ID, updated **only after confirmed placement, confirmed selection, or confirmed upgrade** and invalidated on unknown/changed panel, wrong camera/focus/observer state, any uncertain click, new match, restart, or result appearance. Track it in the same Golden executor, reusing `run_sequence` tower IDs; don't add a second input controller.
- **Same proven target:** when last positively selected ID matches the next action ID, read fresh HUD verifying **Scout name, exact expected level, current count, camera, game state, live cash and quote**. Do **not** move cursor to deselect/reselect; after two stable affordable reads and a final pre-`E` check, send **one `E`**, then require the exact increment/level and constant count.
- **Different target or lost provenance:** validate target point and hover ownership, perform **one guarded direct target selection**, then read back Scout/level/count and verify that the selected panel is causally associated with that known target. HUD type+level alone does not prove unique Scout identity when two Scouts have the same level. If direct switching is not proved native-safe, retain the existing two-click clearing fallback **only in this branch** and only with complete pre/post guards.
- **After placement:** freshly confirmed count+1 and Scout level 0 may establish selection of that *placed ID* only if native post-placement UI supports it; otherwise perform guarded selection. Do not assume hovering a tower means its panel is selected.
- Preserve the existing `hover_check` based non-spending selection retry, but only after **two** fresh ownership/closed-panel observations; never repeat `E` or the placement click on an unconfirmed postcondition.
- Keep single-owner `GuardedInput.check`, F2/F8/Escape, client identity, 1920×991/120DPI, pinned profile SHA/camera and full result-before-input checks.
- Log short, sanitized **`selection_reused`**, **`selection_switched`**, **`selection_fallback`**, and **`selection_invalidated`** events to distinguish effective optimization from a failure; never emit account identifiers, captures, or secrets.

### 2. Result-panel protection and independent recognition (BUG-005)

- Reproduce the saved **selected-Scout-overlay Victory** frame offline. Add a regression first to show that the current `Observer.result_location` and `ResultReader.read` miss the actual TRIUMPH panel.
- Prefer a **safe cursor park** over non-interactive scenery *before the result appears*, only after all 50 actions and with explicit game-state/camera/focus/result checks. A passive pointer move may remove the hover tooltip; if the selected panel still obscures controls, consider one guarded **non-spending** deselect only while definitively in match, never after a visible result.
- Independently harden **read-only result recognition** for the known overlay (current v2.20.4 client): use positive **TRIUMPH/title shape**, verified **Play Again** control, **exact Dead Ahead / Easy / Survival**, **wave 20**, **duration**, and **450 Coin tile** or independently visible result metadata, repeated on two stable frames. The current exact-title fallback is known to fail when `Your Tower` text crosses it. Do not assume a win from `wave=20`, an in-match banner alone, a cached strategy-complete count, or a partial/occluded single label.
- If a result panel is visible but required fields remain occluded, **never send more paid/gameplay input**. Save a sanitized diagnostic, distinguish `result_obscured` from a gameplay timeout, and stop fail-closed promptly rather than spinning for 900 seconds. Keep reward crediting exact-once and derived from positively confirmed result evidence.

### 3. Related backlog without changing unrelated mechanisms

- **BUG-001:** Golden `10/40` recognition excludes an existing exact `count_10.png` pixel template (six prior stops). Fix separately with native saved-frame positive/negative tests; do not synthesize OCR digits.
- **BUG-002:** Fresh camera calibration remains strict. A later session added three recovery starts (two no feature estimate; one 10.49px displacement just beyond 10px), bringing observed camera-start failures to **eight across two Golden sessions**. Diagnose before any bounded correction; **never** widen the 10px placement gate or 2px in-game drift gate.
- **BUG-003:** One Solo badge read failure remains reproduced; retain strict one-vote verification and no Ready click if unreadable.
- A focus-interruption stop is currently expected input-safety behavior, not proof that the foreground guard is buggy. Do not weaken it for convenience.
