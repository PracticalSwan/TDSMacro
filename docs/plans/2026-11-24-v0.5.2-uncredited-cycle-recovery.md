## Proposed behavior (existing strategies only)

1. Separate **result verification**, **credit eligibility**, and **safe continuation**. A failed credit predicate must not automatically mean permanent termination. Preserve strategy-specific requirements, reward verification, action evidence, optional XP placement distinction, and exact-once accounting.
2. For an uncredited but positively identified supported result and independently verified restart control, use the existing guarded same-strategy Restart Match / Play Again or safe return-to-lobby and normal preflight. No click based only on a status string, unreadable UI or guessed coordinates. An unknown/mismatched result must not authorize a result-button click.
3. If a safe direct replay is unavailable, or a bounded replay fails, return a structured **recoverable failure** to the existing supervisor (only when automatic recovery is enabled). Use its verified original-client close/relaunch/lobby route; never retry a paid action or resume a partial match.
4. Preserve the existing two direct retries before escalation and three bounded consecutive guarded relaunches; define a shared consecutive non-credit budget across replay and relaunch so repeated early/mismatched/unreadable results cannot reset the limits or loop forever. Reset on a genuine credited strategy result only. Exact cap proposed: at most two direct replays per failure streak before escalation, at most three client relaunches without credit; no automatic reset at attempt boundaries. If user disables recovery, no relaunch. Explicit stop, exhausted budget, persistent focus loss, wrong process, invalid setup/loadout/economy, storage failure and incompatible strategy remain terminal. Do not blanket-remove `NO_RECOVERY` or convert arbitrary statuses into retries.
5. Record distinct non-credit reason (early wave, late wave, incorrect reward, unreadable receipt, missing required evidence) and attempt identity. Count each non-credit outcome once, keep legitimate totals unchanged, report transition/recovery/terminal reason in the GUI. `early_results` should not silently imply zero early outcomes when an unverified early result occurred: distinguish observed early result from positively confirmed early receipt in diagnostics.
6. Audit both Golden profiles and both current Hardcore variants; preserve one-shot/prepare-only and explicit finite-run semantics. Golden result-overlay detection and this recovery change should integrate without assuming a visible partial result is safely clickable.
